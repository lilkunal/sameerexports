Sand cast, forged, polished and lacquered in Aligarh

Sameer Exports was started in 1994 by Mr. Sanjeev Agarwal under the direction of Er. S.P. Agarwal. Production combines traditional sand casting with modern forging machines, and every piece is finished, polished, lacquered and packed to international buyer standards.
